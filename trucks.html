Our Diverse Fleet

At Apex Transit LLC, we maintain a diverse fleet of trucks to meet various transportation needs. All our vehicles are regularly inspected and maintained to the highest standards to ensure reliability and safety.

Semi-Trailer Trucks

  • Type: Class 8 Semi-Truck
  • Capacity: Up to 80,000 lbs
  • Dimensions: 53' trailer length
  • Fuel Type: Diesel

Our semi-trailer trucks are the backbone of our long-haul operations, equipped with sleeper cabs for driver comfort during extended trips. These trucks feature advanced safety systems including collision avoidance and lane departure warnings.

Box Trucks

  • Type: Class 5-7 Box Truck
  • Capacity: Up to 26,000 lbs
  • Dimensions: 24'-26' box length
  • Fuel Type: Diesel/Gasoline

Ideal for local and regional deliveries, our box trucks offer secure, weatherproof transportation for smaller loads. Features include lift gates for easy loading and unloading, and GPS tracking for real-time shipment monitoring.

Flatbed Trucks

  • Type: Class 8 Flatbed
  • Capacity: Up to 48,000 lbs
  • Dimensions: 48'-53' flatbed
  • Fuel Type: Diesel

Our flatbed trucks are designed for oversized, heavy, or irregularly shaped loads. Equipped with securement systems including chains, binders, and tarps, they're perfect for construction materials, machinery, and other specialized cargo.

Refrigerated Trucks

  • Type: Class 8 Reefer
  • Capacity: Up to 44,000 lbs
  • Dimensions: 48'-53' trailer
  • Temp Range: -20°F to 75°F

Our refrigerated trucks maintain precise temperature control for perishable goods. With advanced monitoring systems, we ensure your temperature-sensitive cargo arrives in perfect condition, whether it's frozen, chilled, or ambient.
