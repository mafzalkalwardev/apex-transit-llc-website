Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

We transport a wide variety of cargo including general freight, perishable goods, construction materials, machinery, and more. We have specialized equipment for different types of cargo including refrigerated trucks for perishables and flatbeds for oversized loads.

We provide transportation services throughout the continental United States. Our primary service areas include the Midwest, Southwest, and East Coast, but we can arrange transportation to any location in the lower 48 states.

We take multiple measures to ensure cargo safety including: GPS tracking on all vehicles, experienced and background-checked drivers, regular vehicle maintenance, proper cargo securement, and comprehensive insurance coverage. We also provide real-time shipment tracking to our clients.

Our standard payment terms are net 30 days from invoice date. We accept all major credit cards, ACH transfers, and checks. For new clients or special circumstances, we may require prepayment or a deposit. Volume discounts are available for regular clients.

You can request a quote by calling our office at (555) 123-4567, emailing quotes@apextransit.com, or filling out the contact form on our website. Please provide details about your shipment including origin, destination, cargo type, weight, dimensions, and any special requirements.

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before scheduled pickup are free of charge. Cancellations within 48 hours may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee. Same-day cancellations or no-shows will be charged the full quoted rate. These terms may vary for specialized equipment or time-sensitive shipments.

Yes, all our trucks are equipped with GPS tracking systems. We provide real-time tracking information to our clients through our online portal. You'll also receive regular status updates from our dispatch team via email or phone, depending on your preference.

We maintain comprehensive liability insurance as required by federal and state regulations. Our cargo insurance covers up to $100,000 per shipment. Additional insurance can be arranged for high-value shipments. Certificates of insurance are available upon request.
